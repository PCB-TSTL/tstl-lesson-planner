Welcome

TSTL Lesson Planner

This app was created by The String Teaching Library (TSTL) and is completely free to use.

It is designed to help music teachers plan lessons, keep track of repertoire, record teaching notes and remember what to continue next time.

Your data stays on your device. Student names, lesson notes, repertoire, practice tasks and other information are stored locally in the browser on the device where you use the app. TSTL does not receive or store this lesson data.
TSTL Free Tool

Lesson Planner features

Student profilesKeep each student’s instrument, level, lesson length and teaching history together.
Lesson planningPlan focus, technique, repertoire, practice tasks, teaching notes and next steps.
Lesson historyReview previous lessons and pick up exactly where you left off.
Practice follow-upReview previous practice tasks as Done, Continue, Not done or Not checked.
Quick NotesSave fast teaching reminders without creating a full lesson plan.
Current and completed repertoireTrack active pieces, completed repertoire and reactivate pieces when needed.
Pinned students and tagsKeep frequently taught students at the top and filter your list using custom tags.
Lesson templatesUse built-in lesson structures or save your own reusable templates.
Progress SnapshotSee a quick overview of repertoire, technique, practice follow-up and next focus.
Print and Save as PDFCreate clean lesson and progress summaries using your browser’s print tools.
Backup and restoreExport your planner data as a backup file and import it again when needed.
Appearance settingsChoose your accent colour and switch between Light and Dark mode.
Privacy: lesson data is stored locally on this device. TSTL does not receive or store your student or lesson information. The app uses a local browser database for reliable offline storage.
